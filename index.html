System One decision stack for Malaysian language — open source, MIT licensed

One model answers many questions. Instead of one specialized classifier per task, a single System One model reads a message once and returns per-option probabilities for every question asked — sentiment, language, urgency, toxicity, emotion — in one forward pass.

Every number on this page is measured on our own held-out native Malay pools, on identical rows for every model, reproducible from raw data in this repository.

The stack

nox_server.py serves any Decision-2.0 model behind the System One envelope. jev_client.py routes trained fields to a millisecond fast tier and untrained questions to the decision tier. finetune_qwen_s17.py is the fine-tune recipe that produced the benchmark row. audit_malaya.py runs the adversarial audit. test_polarity.py is the negation-flip robustness suite.

Honest numbers

Two things we state plainly: our sentiment and emotion columns sit below malaya's 0.994/0.998, because those were earned on their own benchmark text and ours are independent. And our own fine-tune gain was once inflated 10 points by an eval-split leak we caught, measured, and retired — the leak is documented in the season ledger, not buried.
